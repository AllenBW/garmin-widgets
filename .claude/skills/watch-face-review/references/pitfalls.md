# Pitfalls that cost review rounds

Platform and process lessons from the Strata redesign (2026-10-30). Add to
this list when a new one bites.

## Rendering

- **Vector font sizes are heights, not ems.** `Graphics.getVectorFont`'s
  `:size` is the font's whole height, about 1.17 em for Roboto. A Pillow
  preview at the same number shows text about 15 % bigger than the watch.
  Ask the watch for 1.17 times the preview's size (StrataView's
  `EM_HEIGHT` and `font()`), and treat the simulator capture as the
  reference. Shapes (lines, circles, polygons) match exactly.
- **Text has no baseline anchor.** To set a number and its unit on one
  baseline, draw each at `baseline - Graphics.getFontAscent(font)` with
  `TEXT_JUSTIFY_LEFT`.
- **A keyline is text drawn black at eight offsets, then in colour.** That
  cuts big text cleanly out of busy decoration. An outline style is the
  same in reverse: colour at the offsets, black on top. On small text a
  keyline reads as a sticker; give small text its own clear space instead.
- **The moon from `Lib.Moon.draw`** used 8 steps and looked jagged at
  radius 30+; it is 24 now. Always-on's moon needs the terminator as well
  as the lit limb, or a gibbous reads as a crescent.
- **Watchdog:** a few thousand small calls per update (2,000 pass, 5,000
  trip it in the simulator; unit tests have it off). Precompute in
  `designs/gen_*.py`, draw big static things into a full-screen
  `BufferedBitmap` a batch per frame.
- **Pen strokes are centred on the edge on the watch, inside it in
  Pillow.** `drawRoundedRectangle` with a 3 px pen on a 13 px battery
  filled its inside; Pillow's `outline=` stays within the box, so the
  preview looked right. Build small outlined icons from filled shapes
  (shell, black inside, content), inset by whole pixels
  (`Lib.Icons.battery`).
- **Text is placed by advance width, not ink.** Neither `drawText` nor
  Pillow's `anchor` knows a glyph's side bearings, and Roboto
  Condensed's "1" carries 0.155 em on its right. To set a gap by ink,
  measure each digit's bearings from the TTF once and keep a table
  (`Lib.Ink`, generated by `designs/gen_ink.py`); then check a one-digit
  hour and "1"s.
- **Pillow's `textbbox` is the advance box, not the ink.** A preview that
  aligns a number by it puts right-aligned digits their right bearing
  (about 0.017 w for big Bionic) short of where `Lib.Ink` puts them on the
  watch, so the reviewers approve a layout the watch doesn't draw.
  `preview_faces.ink_box` measures the rendered pixels; use it (as
  `place` does) for anything aligned by ink.
- **A vector font's :size per em differs by font.** It is the font's
  ascent plus descent: 1.173 em for Roboto and Roboto Condensed, 1.276
  for Bionic, 1.55 for Pridi. Sizing Bionic with Roboto's 1.17 makes it
  about 9 % small. `Lib.Ink.em` and `Lib.Ink.font` size a font by the
  digits' ink height instead.
- **monkeyc's type checker overflows its stack on one long array literal**
  (around 2,700 numbers). Split generated tables into chunks of 1,000 and
  hand them back from a function (`Field.chunks()`).

- **Settings on the fēnix 8 go through Garmin's watch face editor**, not
  an app menu. The phone app only edits settings for faces installed from
  the store, and `AppBase.getSettingsView` shows nothing for a face on the
  fēnix 8 (only Apply and Delete). Declare `<watchface-config>` in
  `resources/configs/watchface.xml` (styles, accent colours, data
  colours, complication slots; minApiLevel 5.1.0), read it with
  `WatchFaceConfig.getSettings(null)`, and redraw from the delegate's
  `onWatchFaceConfigEdited`. Data slots can only offer Garmin's
  complication types. See the SDK sample `ConfigurableWatchFace`, Strata
  and Rings. `allowAny="true"` (any colour from a picker, any
  complication on the watch, other apps' included) can't be combined
  with a list: monkeyc warns and ignores it. Vessel, Inscription and
  Overlap use `allowAny` for the accent and every slot, fall back to
  their own defaults while a slot is unset, and keep the data colour a
  list. They also offer every choice in the phone app's settings list
  (`designs/gen_face_settings.py` writes both from one description) and
  take whichever was changed last (`Lib.Config`): the editor's settings
  come back non-null with defaults, so "editor over phone" would make
  the phone list do nothing on a fēnix 8.

- **Big digits in one colour need a colon.** A two-tone time reads
  without one, but standby or a bedside clock in a single colour made
  "1 11" read as "111" (Marquee, the face Vessel replaced). Stacked
  hours over minutes need none.

## Design

- Reviewers will ask for things the owner has ruled out; quote the owner
  verbatim in every brief and say what is off the table.
- Things the owner rejected on Strata: segmented sections or boxed panels;
  a full-width line for a reading (heart rate); a word where an icon does
  (the word "SUNSET"); time digits made of hatched decoration ("messy").
- Things that read as UI rather than design: halos round small text, flat
  grey discs with text on them, a filled triangle over a bar (reads as
  "download"), two icons for different things in the same colour or
  shape, uneven icon weights (count inked pixels; aim for a narrow band).
- Clearings in a decoration field: fit them to the measured ink of the
  widest values plus about 5 px, ease them out over about twice their
  height, and keep them as one smooth shape per group. Fully levelled
  clearings look like plaques; a clearing per reading in a row makes
  cells; merging neighbours that are too far apart makes a black funnel.
- Settings: give every colour option a measured distance from the others
  (ΔE of 10 or more) and check every accent on every base.

## Process

- **The shared simulator is one process for the whole Mac.** Claim
  `/tmp/ciq-simulator.lock` (`owner=<id>`, `until=<unix seconds>`) before
  any monkeydo, wait on another owner's live claim, remove it when done
  (`sim-shot.sh` does this). When another session holds it for long,
  start a private one (`scripts/simprivate.sh start`): `test.sh` and
  `sim-shot.sh` use it with no claim.
- **The SDK's monkeydo always reaches the shared simulator.** It ignores
  `$SHELL_SERVER_PORT` and tries ports 1234 to 1238, taking the first that
  answers. A private simulator on another port is only reached through
  `scripts/simprivate.sh do`, which runs monkeydo's Java with a shell that
  rewrites the address. Running plain monkeydo "at" a private simulator
  silently loads your app into someone else's.
- **The simulator allows one instance per home folder** (`~/Sim-<user>`
  holds the running one's process id) and keeps the watch's storage in
  `$TMPDIR/com.garmin.connectiq`; a second one needs its own HOME (with
  links back to `~/Library/Application Support/Garmin` and `~/.garmin`),
  TMPDIR and port, and its app copy needs the SDK's bin files beside it
  (it reads `version.txt` from there) and an ad hoc re-sign after renaming
  its program, or macOS kills it at launch.
- **Silent monkeydo or tests that print nothing means a stale simulator.**
  If it's your private one, `scripts/simprivate.sh stop` and `start`. The
  shared one only while you hold its lock: kill `[M]onkeyDoDeux`, the
  SDK's `bin/shel[l]` and `[C]onnectIQ.app/Contents/MacOS` (one bracketed
  letter, or pkill kills its own shell), start `<sdk>/bin/connectiq`,
  wait 14 s. A by-name kill takes every copy of that program; the private
  one runs as `ciqprivate` so it survives.
- **Capturing the simulator:** find the window with
  `CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo(.optionAll)` (owner "Connect IQ Device
  Simulator", name starting "CIQ Simulator"; with two simulators, match
  the owner's process id: `windows <pid>`), then `screencapture -x -o -l
  <id>`. The id changes after a restart. Never use a bare `screencapture`,
  which grabs the owner's whole screen. Needs the sandbox off. The
  display's place in the window comes from the device's
  `simulator.json` (`display.location` on the device image, which the
  window shows scaled), not from the black around it: the bezel art has
  its own black ring.
- **A fill drawn over an outline's black middle leaves a seam.** The
  fill's anti-aliased edge blends with the black, so a dark line shows
  inside a filled glyph on the watch (Pillow composites masks and hides
  it). Draw the filled part as the outline's colour copies with no black
  middle (Vessel's `outline(..., inside)`).
- **Always-on and Low Power Mode can't be driven** from here (menus need
  Accessibility). Check always-on in the previews and in code, and say so.
- The spacing reviewer measures everything and can take over an hour;
  typography and the skeptic answer in a few minutes. Send narrow changes
  only to the lenses they touch.
