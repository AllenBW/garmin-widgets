# The Connect IQ Store listings

Each app is its own listing, installed from the Connect IQ app on the
phone; Garmin reviews a submission in about three days, and until then
only the developer sees it. The developer display name is **AllenBW**.
Each listing's cover (500 × 500) and screen images (454 × 454, the
simulator's display cut round, from a build with sample readings) are in
`docs/store/`; Blocks, Wall and Lockup lead with a key image that names
every reading (`<face>-key.jpg`, drawn by `designs/store_key.py`), and
carry the same key wide as the listing's hero image (`<face>-hero.jpg`).

## Listings

Category, then the description. Every app is free, with no ads and no
data collected.

**Field** (Watch face). An everyday face: the time large and legible, a
24-hour daylight ring (or steps, or battery), three readings you choose
(heart rate, steps and Body Battery by default), the next sunrise or
sunset, and a row that lights up only for rain ahead, a falling
barometer, a disconnected phone or do not disturb. Press and hold a
reading to open it. To change the look, press and hold the time (the big
numbers). Each hold moves to the next look and shows its name for a
moment where the date is: your own settings, high visibility in orange,
then blue, then green (the ring fluorescent, the text white), low light
for a dark room (ember and cream), a new accent each day, and back to
your own. The Look setting in the phone app picks one too. Built for Garmin's round AMOLED watches (fēnix 8 and 9, epix, Forerunner, Venu, vívoactive and more), with a dim always-on mode that shifts each
minute.

**Daylight** (Watch face). A 24-hour sun dial. The day's light runs round
the edge, with golden hours marked and how much light is left, plus
altitude and the pressure trend (left out while you climb or descend).

**Readiness** (Watch face). Your body metrics without opening a widget:
Body Battery and stress as two gauges round the time, with the sleep
score, recovery time and resting heart rate below it and your training
status at the foot. The sleep score needs a watch on Connect IQ 6 (the
fēnix 8 and 9, Venu 4, Forerunner 570 and 970); the others show it as a
dash. Built for Garmin's round AMOLED watches (fēnix 8 and 9, epix,
Forerunner, Venu, vívoactive and more), with a dim always-on mode that
shows the time and one line of readings.

**Night Sky** (Watch face). The real sky over you right now: the bright
stars, the constellations, the Moon and the planets, worked out on the
watch from your last known position. No phone needed.

**Horizon** (Watch face). The bezel is the next twelve hours: the chance of
rain each hour, dark after sunset, and where the sun sets or rises and
your next calendar event fall.

**Forage** (Watch face). For beekeepers. Whether your bees are flying right
now, the next good hours to open the hive (warm, calm, dry, late morning to
mid-afternoon), the next twelve hours as a strip of comb, and the
beekeeper's year along the bottom of the bezel. Uses the hourly forecast
from your phone; it says nothing rather than guess when the weather is
hours old. The time is in 24-hour form. The nectar calendar follows the
mid-Atlantic United States (Virginia, Maryland); elsewhere the seasons
along the bezel will be early or late.

**Today** (Watch face). One plain list and nothing else: the next sunrise
or sunset, the time in 24-hour form, the date, the moon and its phase,
heart rate, the outside temperature (in the watch's unit), distance walked
today and any alarms set. No settings.

**Strata** (Watch face). The day's readings set into a field of soil
layers that part round each one like water round a stone. The time is
cut cleanly out of the layers, a stone turns up for every quarter mile
walked, and the moon sits at the foot. Choose the colours and what each
of the four readings shows (temperature, rain, heart rate, distance,
Body Battery, elevation, humidity, wind, battery or steps). To change
the look, press and hold the time (the big numbers). Each hold moves to
the next look and shows its name for a moment where the date is: your
own settings, high visibility in orange, then blue, then green (the
layers dimmed, the time fluorescent, the text white), low light for a
dark room (soil, ember and sandstone), a new look each day, and back to
your own. The Look setting in the phone app picks one too.

**Rings** (Watch face). Three goal rings open to the right (steps,

**Clack** (Watch face). A split-flap clock with no case: four leaves, one

digits as outlines and shifts each minute.

**Battery Planner** (Widget). Tells you when to charge. It records the
battery every half hour in the background, learns your drain over the last
week and projects the day you will need the charger. The glance shows the
level and the days left; open it for the week day by day, the pace and
the charge-by date. Until it has a day of readings it shows the watch's
own estimate. Built for Garmin's round AMOLED watches (fēnix 8 and 9,
epix, Forerunner, Venu, vívoactive and more).

**Sun and Moon** (Widget). The next sunrise or sunset, golden and blue
hour, and the moon's phase, worked out on the watch, offline.

**Sun Compass** (Widget). A compass rose that turns with the watch, with

**Sauna and Steam** (Health and fitness). Times a sauna or steam-room
visit round by round. Your heart-rate recovery is measured after each
round, the coaching follows the published guidance, every button has its
job written beside it, and the visit is saved to Garmin Connect. Rounds
start and end on their own: the watch's thermometer notices when it warms
as you go in and cools as you come out. To mark the door yourself, turn
off "Notice going in", "Notice going out" or both in the app's settings on
your phone; LAP marks the door by hand at any time.

**Tally** (Tools). Buttons you make, one tap to log, and the pattern
they draw. Set up to twelve buttons on your phone (Garmin Connect, the
app's settings): a name, a colour, normal or large, and the hours each
shows, so the morning's buttons give way to the evening's. Buttons can
be added, renamed, recoloured, resized and removed on the watch too
(All, then Buttons). On the watch they sit in a ring; a tap logs the
moment with a buzz, and a tap in the middle within six seconds takes it
back. The All list has every button, every name used in the last 90
days, and Other for anything unexpected, typed on the watch; add an
entry you missed earlier, or open History to change or delete one. Swipe
for the last two weeks: a ring per day with each entry a dot at its
time, so a habit lines up as a spoke ("usually 7:55, 12 of 14 days"); a
strip of what came before; and which buttons tend to come before one,
against the days it didn't happen. The glance shows the newest entry and
how long ago. Optionally every entry also goes to a Google Sheet of your
own, with a summary for sharing (setup guide: github.com/AllenBW/garmin-
widgets/tree/main/apps/tally/sheet). The entries stay on your watch and
in your own sheet; nothing else is collected. It shows what happened
together, not what caused what, and is not a medical device.

**Stretching God** (Health and fitness). Guided stretching with a coach
on your wrist. Thirty-nine stretches, each drawn as a figure that eases
in, holds and comes out again with the muscle being stretched in orange,
strung into twelve routines: morning, a quick five minutes, a desk break
with no floor, before bed, a full-body session, and a warm-up or
cool-down for a run, a ride, a hike or lifting. The glance and the
opening card suggest the one that suits now, from your wake and sleep
times, the move bar and your last activity. Each stretch is shown whole
before it starts, then counted in; while it runs you get the coach's
line, the time left and the hold round the bezel, and a buzz with its own
meaning for each turn (go, ten seconds left, release, switch sides, next
stretch, done), taught in a short tutorial so you can stretch without
looking at the watch. Holds come in three lengths; contract-relax is
optional in the full-body and after-activity routines. DOWN eases you out
of anything that hurts and moves on. Routines are saved to Garmin Connect
as Flexibility Training with a lap per stretch, and your streak is kept
on the watch. The routines follow the published guidance on stretching
(about a minute a muscle, moving stretches before activity, nothing
promised about injury or soreness). Educational use only, not medical
advice or treatment: stop if anything hurts, and check with a clinician
first if you are injured, pregnant or recovering from surgery.

**Sound Level** (Tools). Experimental. An approximate sound level meter
from the watch microphone: the level in decibels coloured by how loud it
is, NIOSH's daily limit at that level (85 dB for 8 hours, half the time
for every 3 dB more), and a dose while the app is open. Past what the
microphone can read it says "too loud to measure" rather than showing a
number that is too low. It listens only while open, and never records,
stores or sends audio. Not a calibrated instrument and not a medical
device; calibrate it against a phone meter from the menu. If the
microphone stops sending sound, press BACK and open the app again.
